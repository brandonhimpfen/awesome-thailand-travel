## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, visa requirements, and weather tracking.

- [Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT - Amazing Thailand)](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring regional destination highlights, cultural itineraries, and travel guides.
- [Thailand Immigration Bureau](https://www.immigration.go.th/) - Official government portal for visa regulations, electronic travel authorization (ETA), and border entry rules.
- [Thai Meteorological Department (TMD)](https://www.tmd.go.th/en) - Official national meteorological agency providing weather forecasts, seasonal monsoon updates, and regional climate tracking.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating national railway networks, high-speed rail lines, domestic aviation, and ferry services.

- [State Railway of Thailand (SRT)](https://www.railway.co.th/) - Official national railway operator managing intercity train routes connecting Bangkok with Chiang Mai, Surat Thani, and border regions.
- [Thai Airways International](https://www.thaiairways.com/) - National flag carrier operating international flights and domestic connections through Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK).
- [AirAsia Thailand](https://www.airasia.com/) - Major domestic low-cost airline providing frequent regional connections across Thai airports and island hubs.
- [Lomprayah High Speed Ferries](https://www.lomprayah.com/) - Premier catamaran ferry service connecting mainland Surat Thani and Chumphon with Koh Samui, Koh Phangan, and Koh Tao.

## Destinations & Regions

### Bangkok & Central Thailand

- [Bangkok Metropolitan Administration Tourism Portal](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) - Official visitor guide for exploring the Grand Palace, Wat Arun, historic temples, and floating markets.
- [Bangkok Mass Transit System (BTS Skytrain)](https://www.bts.co.th/) - Official rapid transit operator guide for navigating Bangkok's elevated rail network.

### Chiang Mai & Northern Highlands

- [Chiang Mai Tourism Organization](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) - Official regional portal for exploring Old Town temples, night bazaars, Doi Suthep, and surrounding mountain trekking reserves.

### Phuket & Andaman Coast

- [Phuket Provincial Tourism Office](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) - Official visitor guide for Patong, Old Phuket Town, and Andaman Sea island-hopping excursions.
- [Similan Islands National Park Portal](https://portal.dnp.go.th/) - Official Department of National Parks guide for premier diving and marine conservation sites in the Andaman Sea.

### Samui & Gulf Islands

- [Tourism Thailand - Gulf Islands Guide](https://www.tourismthailand.org/) - Official regional hub for exploring Koh Samui beach resorts, Koh Phangan full moon heritage, and Koh Tao scuba diving sites.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Thailand's Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) and remote work programs.

- [Thailand Destination Thailand Visa (DTV) Portal (MFA)](https://www.mfa.go.th/) - Official government consular portal detailing long-term remote work visa requirements, digital nomad eligibility, and permitted stay lengths.
- [Punspace Chiang Mai Coworking](https://www.punspace.com/) - Popular community workspace and hub for remote professionals located in Chiang Mai.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on tourist police assistance, medical emergency lines, and local safety rules.

- [Thailand Tourist Police (Dial 1155)](https://www.touristpolice.go.th/) - Dedicated 24-hour police division offering multi-language assistance, safety patrols, and support for international visitors.
- [General Emergency Hotline (Police 191 / Medical Ambulance 1669)](https://www.bangkok.com/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all Thai provinces.
